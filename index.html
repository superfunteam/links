How it goes

The rules

We give you every first letter, you solve the word chain. Each word links the one below: sometimes a compound word like OVERHEAD, sometimes a before-and-after pair like PARTY ANIMAL.

Guesses cost 1 stroke. A miss gifts you the next letter, and you can buy a letter for 1 stroke. Golf rules, low score wins.
